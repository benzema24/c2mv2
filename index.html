Interactive preview

Four proposed enhancements to Alliant's C2M platform

Today we're walking through an interactive preview of the four enhancements proposed for Alliant's C2M platform: bulk subcontractor requests from an Excel import, client-specific questionnaires built from a shared base template, financial data extraction from PDF statements into Excel, and customizable reporting.

1Requests from ExcelUpload, validate, detect duplicates, review, send invitations
2QuestionnairesBase template with independent client-specific templates
3FinancialsPDF statements extracted, validated, reviewed, exported to Excel
4ReportsFilter rows, pick columns, preview, export exactly that

This is a demonstration prototype, not the live system. The data is sample data and backend operations are simulated. Everything on screen responds to clicks the way the real system would; where a step would normally reach the server, you'll see a blue "simulated" label explaining what happens in production.

As we go, I'd like your reaction to three things: whether each workflow matches how your team actually works, anything that feels missing or unnecessary, and the business rules we've flagged as open questions. Let's start where your users start, in the Portfolio.
